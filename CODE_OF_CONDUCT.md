# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participation in the AYRN project — issues, discussions, and (once open) code contributions — is expected to be professional and respectful. This applies to everyone regardless of experience level, background, or role.

## Expected Behavior

- Give and accept constructive technical criticism without personal attacks.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before assuming bad intent.
- Disagree on technical grounds, and be willing to change your mind when shown a better argument.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks directed at other participants.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or issue threads.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening an issue marked confidential or, once one exists, emailing the address listed in the repository's contact information. Maintainers will review reports and take action proportionate to the violation, up to and including removal from the project.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
